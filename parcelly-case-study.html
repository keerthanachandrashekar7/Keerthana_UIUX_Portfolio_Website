PARCELLY.

A smart parcel management app concept designed to make apartment deliveries easier to manage, schedule, and track — all in one place.

ROLEUI/UX Designer
PROJECT TYPEConcept / Mobile App
PLATFORMiOS · Mobile
TOOLSFigma
01 / OVERVIEW

One place for every parcel.

Apartment residents often receive deliveries when they are away, miss updates, or need to coordinate with a society desk. PARCELLY explores a clearer way to view incoming parcels and arrange convenient delivery.

Design goal

Make parcel status easy to understand and make the next action — pickup, scheduling, or tracking — obvious to residents.

02 / PROBLEM & OPPORTUNITY

The challenge

Parcel information can be spread across messages, calls, delivery apps, and society staff. Residents may not know whether a parcel has arrived or how to receive it.

The opportunity

Bring parcel status, delivery preferences, scheduling, and delivery verification into one simple resident experience.

03 / CORE USER FLOW

From arrival to doorstep.

Sign in→Select apartment→View parcel→Choose delivery→Schedule→Track→Verify→Delivered

The flow keeps the main task focused: find a parcel, choose how and when to receive it, and follow its progress.

04 / VISUAL DESIGN

A focused, high-contrast interface.

The visual direction uses a dark foundation with warm orange accents to make primary actions easy to spot. Cards group related information and a consistent type scale supports quick scanning.

Background · #101010
Cards · #1C1C1C
Primary · #FFAA35
Text · #FFFFFF
Secondary · #A1A1A1
05 / SCREEN INVENTORY

25 screens across the resident journey.

01 — 04

Splash and onboarding: introduce the product and its value.

05 — 08

Account creation, login, OTP, and apartment selection.

09 — 11

Home dashboard, parcel list, and parcel details.

12 — 17

Delivery option, address, schedule, time slot, summary, and payment.

18 — 20

Booking confirmation, live tracking, and delivery OTP.

21 — 22

Delivery success and experience rating.

23 — 25

Delivery history, notifications, and profile settings.

06 / REFLECTION

Designed around the next useful action.

PARCELLY is a concept project. The next design step would be to validate the flow with apartment residents and society staff, test the scheduling experience, and refine the information shown at each delivery stage.
